IMPORTANT DATA PROVENANCE NOTICE

The CSV in data/raw/gpotsim_reconstructed.csv was generated by src/simulate.py from the manuscript's described setup. It is a deterministic reconstruction, not an original dataset recovered from the authors' earlier experiment. It has seven attack categories and three operating modes. It is provided to make the implementation executable and inspectable. Do not describe it as real industrial telemetry or as the exact original GP-OTSim raw data. Manuscript-reported metrics are separately labelled and are not overwritten by this package's computed metrics.
